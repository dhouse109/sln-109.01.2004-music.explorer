109 Studios is a Music Promotion company that hosts music from Artists, DJ’s, and Online Radio Stations for promotion in our online community.  The 109 Studios Explorer is a Music Portal that gives members the ability to legally download free music, find music through word-of-mouth, and promote their favorite artists.

Established in March of 2004, 109 Studios was inspired by a love for music and sharing it with people.  We set out to merge the power of the Internet, social networks, and digital music to create a community that was all about music -- the people who love it, and the artists that create it.
